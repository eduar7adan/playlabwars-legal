Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 · 2 September 2026

Controller and contact

PlayLabWars is operated by PlayLabStudio, the developer account shown as the app owner in Reddit. Contact u/PlayLabStudio for privacy support.

Data needed to play

For a scoring game, Reddit provides the app with your internal account identifier, public Reddit username, and community. The app stores the identifier, public username, dates, game identifiers, attempt count, and daily and weekly results to recover games, prevent duplicates, and create leaderboards.

We do not store email, avatar, messages, posts, comments, or tap timing data. Practice stores no results and never adds leaderboard points.

Leaderboards and storage

Individual data is stored in Reddit Redis. Player leaderboards display public Reddit usernames and scores, never internal identifiers.

When the shared league is enabled, Supabase receives only subreddit name, period, player count, total points, revision, and expiry. It never receives player identifiers, usernames, or individual scores.

Retention and deletion

Individual results remain until 14 days after their week closes and never more than 21 days from play. Use Rules & privacy → Delete my data in this community to remove retained results and subtract their points. This cannot be undone; repeat in any other community where you played.

Changes

We will update this page and in-game information if these practices change.
