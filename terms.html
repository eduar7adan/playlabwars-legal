PlayLabWars is an independent game offered by PlayLabStudio. It is not an official Reddit app. Reddit’s terms, policies, and the rules of the community where you play also apply.

Participation

A valid Reddit account is required to save points. Practice does not score. Games last 30 seconds after a three-second lead-in. Only the first tap in each round counts. Correct targets score 100 to 145 points by speed; taps in the first 90 ms do not score.

You may replay games. Only the best result for each day and community adds points. A week adds up to seven daily scores and runs in UTC. Leaving does not stop the clock. Player leaderboards display each participant’s public Reddit username and team leaderboards display the community name.

There are no purchases, prizes, betting, or monetary value attached to scores.

Fair play

Do not use automation or accounts to manipulate results. Do not access other people’s data, harass other communities, or promote attacks. Report bugs or abuse to u/PlayLabStudio or the community moderators.

Availability and data

The service depends on Reddit and, when enabled, the shared league connection. It may change, be interrupted, or be withdrawn. See the Privacy Policy and the in-game Rules & privacy screen before playing.
